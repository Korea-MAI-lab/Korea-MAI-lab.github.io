# Research at MAI Lab

## 1. Photoacoustic & Ultrasound Imaging
We develop acoustic and hybrid imaging platforms that provide structural, functional, and molecular information. Topics include volumetric imaging, multimodal acquisition, system optimization, and quantitative analysis.

## 2. Radiation-Induced Acoustic Imaging
We investigate acoustic waves generated by radiation energy deposition, with interests in radiation imaging, dose monitoring, high-sensitivity detection, and physics-based reconstruction.

## 3. AI & Quantitative Imaging
We combine model-based imaging physics with deep learning for image reconstruction, artifact reduction, segmentation, multimodal registration, uncertainty analysis, and quantitative biomarker extraction.

## 4. Biomedical Applications
We translate the above technologies to disease monitoring, treatment-response assessment, biodynamics, and preclinical/clinical collaborations.
