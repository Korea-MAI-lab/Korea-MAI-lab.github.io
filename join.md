# We are looking for curious builders.

MAI Lab welcomes students interested in biomedical imaging, acoustics, radiation science, signal/image processing, system development, and AI.

## Potential project tracks

- Imaging system development (hardware + software)
- Acoustic / photoacoustic / radiation-acoustic signal processing
- AI-enhanced reconstruction and image analysis
- Preclinical and translational biomedical applications

## How to apply

Send a short introduction, CV, research interests, and the type of project you would like to explore to **YOUR_EMAIL@korea.ac.kr**.
