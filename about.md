# Multimodal Acoustic Imaging Laboratory

MAI Lab explores how acoustic waves can be used as a common sensing language across biomedical imaging modalities. We develop imaging systems and computational methods that connect physical energy deposition, acoustic propagation, image reconstruction, and quantitative biological interpretation.

## Our approach

We work across the full imaging pipeline: **hardware → acquisition → reconstruction → AI analysis → quantitative validation → biomedical application**.

## Lab culture

We value rigorous measurement, reproducible computation, open collaboration, and research that connects fundamental imaging physics with useful biomedical questions.
